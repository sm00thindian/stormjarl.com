About

StormJarl is Dark Nordic Blues — the weight of a modern rock riff colliding with the ancient ritual pulse of the North.

Thundering, snarling low end you feel in your chest. War drums and chanted voices that sound like they rose out of a fjord at night. Cold wind, raven calls and the crackle of frost wrapped around everything. It is primal and cinematic: music for sagas, for long winter drives, for the end of the world.

The story began with the debut EP Blood On the Runes, told by a saga-teller’s voice that moves from half-spoken verse to a full-throated roar. In 2026, Ásdís Kjartandóttir’s crystalline, otherworldly voice joined the storm — two perspectives, fire and ice, answering each other across a run of singles and the Live In The Stone Circle EP.

With Midnight Circuit, the old gods found their way into the machine. Next comes Level Up, with Ásdís leading the way.

For fans of Royal Blood and Wardruna. Out of Zoe, Oklahoma, on Bridge Recording.
